What Funngro is

A place to find work that fits around your life.

Funngro connects young Indians with opportunities from brands and businesses. You can pick up work that matches your skills and time, build real experience and get paid for what you deliver.

Everything runs through one app: browse, apply, submit, track and get paid.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version of how the platform works.

What is Funngro? +

Funngro is a platform where young Indians can find opportunities from brands and businesses, build experience and get paid for the work they deliver.

What kind of opportunities are available? +

Work is organised around four broad types — content, brand promotion, referrals and smaller tasks. What's available depends on what brands and businesses are running at the time.

How does the process work? +

You browse available work, apply for what fits your skills, complete the requirements and submit your work. Once the campaign's requirements are met, payment is processed.

How do payments work? +

Payment is processed according to the terms of each campaign. Exact timing and method depend on the individual opportunity.

How do I get started? +

Download the Funngro app and set up your profile. From there you can browse what's available and apply for work that matches your skills.
